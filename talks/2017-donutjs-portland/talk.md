## What happened

Donut.js was the Portland meetup with donuts. This is the only recording under half an hour, and
its captions are the cleanest of the set. YouTube even punctuated this one.

From the recording, near the top: "Full disclosure. I've given this talk about five times at
different conferences. It's been a little different each time. It's usually 45 minutes to an hour. I
made an effort to pare it down. I'll confess... I don't know how good of a job I did. So if I start
speaking really quickly and this ends really abruptly, please bear with me."

The deck has a commit on the same day, 2017-09-26, "Added logos for chrome". The DonutJS channel
posted the video on 2017-10-01.
