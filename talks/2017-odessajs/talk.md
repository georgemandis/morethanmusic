## What happened

The second outing, a month after HolyJS, and the first time I gave two talks at one conference. The
MIDI talk "chocked-full of demos and code samples" and an eight-year retrospective on Konami-JS.
That is from the post announcing it, 2017-06-13. It also says I was looking forward to familiar faces
from St. Petersburg, and that the MIDI talk would be given "a number of times in the coming months."
It was.

The projector only had VGA. I had not anticipated that. Someone in the room had a USB-C to VGA
adapter and saved the MIDI talk. I could not find that person again for the Konami talk, so ten
minutes before going on I tunneled the reveal.js deck through ngrok and ran it from an older laptop
that still had a working VGA adapter. The post about that, 2017-07-06, is also where the photo of
the whole rig lives: six controllers, three Node servers, one USB-C port.

The OdesaJS channel posted the recording on 2017-07-16. It is the longest one of the lot at 61
minutes.
