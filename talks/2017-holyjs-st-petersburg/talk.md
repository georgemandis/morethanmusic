## What happened

The first outing of Make More Than Music with Tiny Computers, JavaScript and MIDI, an hour long, on
the last day. Six MIDI controllers, three Node servers tunneled through ngrok, a WebRTC demo, a
WebSockets demo, all off a 2015 MacBook with one USB-C port. The room joined a wifi network called
Sputnik and opened sputnik.local, and I played their phones from a keyboard. The Puck.js in my hand
sent middle C over Bluetooth to advance the slides.

I flew close to 30 hours to get there and left my voltage adapters at home. Lea Verou and Douglas
Crockford were on the same bill. The HolyJS channel posted the recording on 2017-11-22. It runs 56
minutes.
