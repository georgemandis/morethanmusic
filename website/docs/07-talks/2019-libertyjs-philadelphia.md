## What happened

The site for this one is the SPA site with a new coat of paint. Its repo starts on 2019-10-25,
"initial commit", with typo fixes on 2019-11-05 and 2019-11-14. The home page is retitled
"JavaScript + MIDI + Microcontrollers Workshop" and the agenda adds Microsoft Edge to
Chrome, Chromium and Opera. Firefox was still "in the pipeline". The
rest, the ten modes, the project list, the CodePen starters, is word for word the 2018 page, with
the typos fixed.

One detail for the archaeologists: the deploy script still pushes to `spa2018-midi-workshop.git`, so
this site replaced the SPA one at the same GitHub Pages URL. Both are offline now, and neither was
captured by the Wayback Machine. This site's pages are re-hosted below.

The talks page has LibertyJS on October 24 and 25, 2019. The midi.mand.is landing page lists it as
the most recent place the workshop was led.
