## What happened

A month after HolyJS, and the first time I gave two talks at one conference: the MIDI talk and an
eight-year retrospective on Konami-JS. The projector only had VGA. Someone in the room lent a USB-C
to VGA adapter for the MIDI talk. For the Konami talk I tunneled the deck through ngrok and ran it
from an older laptop that still had a VGA adapter.

The recording, 61 minutes, is the longest of the set.
